# Original 25 Pocket God Comics — source recovery and fidelity gate

**Current status: original issue-page source recovery is incomplete.** The project has 25 original issue covers and 25 reconstructed story archives derived from the creators' WEBTOON republication. It does **not yet** have a verified library of the 25 original individually purchased iVerse issue-page files.

A reconstructed story and an original publisher-delivered digital issue are **not automatically identical**. The source editions can differ in page layout, title pages, publisher letters, credits, advertisements, or bonus material. We will not call a release "original 25 definitive" based only on WEBTOON copies.

## Publication history and legitimate source routes

- **2010 Ape physical issues #1–4.** Original separate printed issues were published. [MyComicShop issue listings](https://www.mycomicshop.com/search?TID=21311425&mingr=0) provide physical-source references. Original issue scan or publisher-supplied original files would still need rights validation before distribution.
- **Pocket God Preview Edition #0.** Official promotional physical preview, distinct from numbered story issues. [Ape publisher catalog listings](https://www.mycomicshop.com/search?pl=Ape+Entertainment).
- **Collected editions.** *The Gem of Life*, *A Tale of Two Pygmies* and *A Quest Called Tribe* have collected editions. These can help verify original panel/page layout and story boundaries but should not be treated as complete coverage of issues #1–25. [Book listings](https://www.mycomicshop.com/search?SeriesID=22856123).
- **Original digital issues through iVerse.** Contemporary reporting documents in-app purchases of individual issues. A **user-owned historical app purchase backup**, a legitimate publisher master, or a rights-holder supplied copy is the preferred source for digital issue authenticity.
- **Publisher previews and release coverage.** Documentation and selected authorized preview pages are useful comparison references, not substitutes for complete original comic issues. [Issue #24 publisher preview and announcement](https://www.brokenfrontier.com/exclusive-preview-for-the-daring-pygmie-desert-escape-in-pocket-god-24/).

The first four issues were published separately in print; the continuing 25-issue series became predominantly digital. Calling all 25 "print issues" was imprecise. They are **the original numbered issues**, distinct from the WEBTOON chapters.

## Source acceptance gates

1. **Provenance.** Record where a particular issue was acquired; don't silently relabel WEBTOON chapters as original original-iVerse issues.
2. **Completeness.** Check individual issue page count, title/issue number, cover source, page continuity and any original publisher credit/bonus pages.
3. **Integrity.** Decode images, hash every source page, detect duplicates and unexpected missing/low-resolution pages.
4. **Artwork.** Compare newly supplied high-res cover art against the correct **issue or chapter** number and associated story; avoid mismatching print covers and WEBTOON posts.
5. **Legacy QA.** Confirm original My Comics navigation, 25 numbered issues, correct story order and readable pages on iPhone 4, 4S and iPad.
6. **Release rights.** The public repository may host project-authored compatibility source and documentation. Distribution of modified proprietary iVerse binaries and commercial comic pages requires appropriate authorization.
7. **Modern ports.** After legacy provenance and hardware QA, the same accepted issue manifest becomes the sole content source for modern 64-bit iOS and Android builds.

The existing v16 WEBTOON-derived reconstruction is a **temporary comparison/reference build**, not the final authenticity benchmark. The separate 63-chapter creator WEBTOON project remains a later phase.

## What would unblock a true original issue build?

Verified original digital issue archives from an authorized purchaser or the rights holders, originals from appropriately sourced print editions/collections, and the newly downloaded high-resolution art files once staged and mapped. The project never auto-publishes those source files to a public repository.
