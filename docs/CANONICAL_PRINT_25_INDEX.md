# Original Pocket God numbered issues — provisional WEBTOON story reconstruction

> **Provenance warning:** This is a *story reconstruction* from creator WEBTOON chapters, not evidence that the project holds the original separately sold iVerse issue files. The 25 entries are original numbered *digital/print series issues*; not every numbered issue had a separately printed physical release. See [original issue source recovery](ORIGINAL_SOURCE_RECOVERY.md).

These are the **25 original numbered issues**, **not** the first 25 WEBTOON posts.
The private v16 story rebuild combines chapters that the creators later split when republishing the comic on WEBTOON.
No commercial pages are distributed in this repository.

## Items not part of the original numbered print-issue stories

- **WEBTOON chapters #9–12:** four standalone mini-stories.
- **Chapter #33:** WEBTOON recap.
- **Chapter #53:** creator update/apology.
- **Chapter #60:** creator apology.
- **Chapters #61–62:** issue **26A/26B**, a later creator continuation beyond the original 25 print issues.
- **Chapter #63:** creator update.

These 10 items are **not discarded**: all remain in the complete WEBTOON 63-chapter edition at their official chapter numbers.

**What is verified:** original comic/story segment ordering, the print issue titles/arcs, original issue #1–25 covers and the byte-identical 629 WEBTOON-republished story images.

**Not established:** a bit-for-bit facsimile of original printed pages, advertisements, lettering/bonus page layouts and print-specific editorial materials. These were not independently recovered. The builder never invents missing print pages.
